Dot&Boxes Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Summary

dot&boxes does not ask you to create an account. Your game progress stays on your device. Personal data leaves your device only when you play online (handled by Google Firebase), through Google's advertising service (AdMob), and by Apple or Google when you make a purchase.

This policy covers the dot&boxes app for iPhone, iPad and Android. It explains what is stored, what is shared, and the choices you have.

Who we are

dot&boxes is developed and published by Taiwo Peace, who is the data controller for any personal data processed through the app. "We", "us" and "our" in this policy mean Taiwo Peace.

For any privacy question or request, email olorundaolaoluwa@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Online play (Google Firebase)

If you choose to play online, the game uses Google Firebase to find opponents and run matches. Only then does it:

  • sign you in with an anonymous Firebase account (a random ID, with no email, phone number or password)
  • store the name and avatar you chose for online play, which your opponents see
  • store match data: the board size, the moves played, whether a player left, rematch requests, and when each player's app last checked in during a match
  • briefly store your place in the matchmaking queue, open challenges you send, and pairing codes you create

We use this data only to run online matches. Other players can see your online name and avatar, and the moves of matches you play with them; nobody else can see your matches. Waiting-list entries, challenges and codes are removed when you stop searching or the match starts. Firebase processes this data under Google's Firebase privacy terms. If you never open the Online mode, none of this is created.

Please don't use your real full name or anything personal as your online name. To have your online data deleted, email us (see "Who we are").

Advertising (Google AdMob)

dot&boxes is free to play and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. We show banner ads during matches, full-screen ads between matches, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Apple IDFA, where you allow tracking, or Android Advertising ID)
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped
  • diagnostic and performance data, such as crash and load-time information

Google uses this data to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you have agreed, personalise ads. Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that show its ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We have set the app to request only ads rated suitable for general audiences (G). We do not receive your advertising identifier or any personal data from Google; we only see aggregated reports, such as the number of ads shown.

Consent and your choices

You control how ads use your data, and you can change your mind at any time.

  • Consent form (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required). On first launch, the app shows Google's consent form, provided through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept or decline personalised ads. To change your choice later, open Settings → Privacy settings in the game.
  • Apple tracking permission (iPhone and iPad). The app asks once whether it may track you across apps for advertising. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Remove Ads. A one-time in-app purchase turns off banner and between-match ads. Optional rewarded ads stay available if you choose to watch them.

If you decline personalised ads, you will still see ads, but they will be based on general context rather than your activity.

In-app purchases

Coin packs and Remove Ads are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment, under their own privacy policies. We never see your name, email address, card number or other payment details.

After a purchase, the store tells the app that the purchase succeeded and gives it a transaction ID. The app keeps that ID on your device only, so the same purchase is never credited twice.

Reminder notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, such as when your daily reward is ready. They are created and stored on your device only; no push-notification server is used and no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game under Settings → Daily reminders, or in your device's notification settings.

Children's privacy

dot&boxes is a general-audience game and is not directed primarily at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at olorundaolaoluwa@gmail.com and we will help you have it deleted.

Retention, deletion and security

  • On your device: game data stays until you delete it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it permanently. We cannot restore it, because we never hold a copy.
  • Google AdMob: Google keeps advertising data for the periods described in its Privacy Policy. Resetting your advertising ID breaks the link between past and future ad data.
  • Purchases: Apple and Google keep purchase records under their own policies. Bought items can be restored on a new device with Settings → Restore purchases in the game.
  • International transfers: Google may process advertising data in countries other than yours, including the United States, using safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Online play: match records are kept for up to 90 days so finished games and rematches work, then deleted. Delete requests are handled within 30 days.
  • Security: all data is encrypted in transit. Online match data is protected by access rules so that only the two players in a match can read or change it.

Your rights

EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. We rely on your consent for personalised advertising, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and measurement. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

California and other US states. You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Sharing with Google for personalised ads may count as "sharing" under California law; you can opt out by declining personalised ads in the consent form, choosing "Ask App Not to Track" on iOS, or opting out of ads personalisation on Android.

To exercise any right, email olorundaolaoluwa@gmail.com. Because we hold no account data, most requests about advertising data are best made directly to Google, at myaccount.google.com or through its privacy help page. We will help where we can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change materially affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
