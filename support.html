Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Take turns drawing a line between two neighbouring dots. Close the fourth side of a box to claim it and take another turn. When every box is claimed, the player with the most boxes wins. Tap Learn on the home screen for an interactive tutorial.

I bought coins or Remove Ads but don't see them.

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play; if a payment went through but nothing arrived after restoring, email us with the date and your order ID from your receipt.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone and iPad, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open the Google Play Store, go to Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, and choose the purchase.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Your game progress is saved on your device. Purchases can be restored on a new device with Settings → Restore purchases. Achievements and leaderboard scores are kept in Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android).

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where required, open Settings → Privacy settings in the game. On iPhone and iPad, tracking can also be changed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off banner and between-match ads.

How do I turn off reminders?

Open Settings → Daily reminders in the game, or turn off notifications for dot&boxes in your device settings.

The music or sound won't play.

Check Settings → Sound effects and Binaural music in the game, your device volume, and (on iPhone) the silent switch.
